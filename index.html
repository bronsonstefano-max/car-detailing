Our Services

Our high-end detailing services are built around one mission: Correction, Protection, Reflection. Our technicians bring decades of combined experience and a deep familiarity with a wide range of paint systems — from gloss enhancement, to cutting through hard-water etching and scratches, we have the team and tools to bring your vehicle back to its true color. We install paint protection film in both traditional clear and satin finishes, as well as colored films, and offer professional-grade ceramic coatings and ceramic window tint for maximum cabin cooling and protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does paint protection film last?

High-quality PPF typically carries a 10-year warranty when installed by certified installers.

Is ceramic coating worth it?

Yes — it adds gloss, chemical resistance and makes washing dramatically easier.

How long will my car be in the shop?

Tint is usually same-day, ceramic coatings 2–3 days, and full PPF 3–5 days.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes. Coatings, film and tint are all backed by manufacturer and shop warranties.
