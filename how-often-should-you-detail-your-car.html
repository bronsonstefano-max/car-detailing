How Often Should You Detail Your Car in Miami?

A practical maintenance schedule for a car that lives outdoors.

October 2026 · By the Divine Detailers team

There is no single answer, because it depends on how you use the car and where it parks. This is a sensible baseline for a daily driver in South Florida.

Washing

  • Every one to two weeks, or sooner after bugs, birds or heavy rain and dust.
  • Use a safe method: pre-rinse, pH-neutral soap, a clean mitt and a proper dry.

Interior cleaning

  • Quick vacuum and wipe down every few weeks.
  • A deeper interior detail every three to six months: vacuum, steam or shampoo fabric, clean and condition leather, treat plastics and clean the glass.
  • More often with kids, pets or food in the car.

Exterior detail and protection

  • A full exterior detail with decontamination (clay and iron removal) and fresh sealant or wax about two to four times a year if you do not have a coating.
  • A ceramic coating reduces how often you need to do this. A yearly inspection and refresh is still smart.

Paint correction

  • Only when needed, since it removes a thin layer of clear coat. Fix swirls and scratches once, then protect the paint so they do not return.

Glass, wheels and trim

  • Clean glass whenever it is dirty. A glass sealant helps rain bead off.
  • Clean wheels with a product made for your wheel finish.
  • Treat rubber and exterior trim with a UV protectant every few months.

Adjust for your situation

  • Parked outside or near the coast: more often.
  • Garage kept and lightly driven: less often.
  • New car: protect it early with coating or film, so the paint never gets damaged.

Regular light care is cheaper and easier than a big restoration after neglect.
