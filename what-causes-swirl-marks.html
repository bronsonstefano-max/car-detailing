What Causes Swirl Marks, and How to Remove Them

Why swirls appear, how paint correction removes them, and how to avoid them.

October 2026 · By the Divine Detailers team

Swirl marks are the fine, circular scratches you see on paint in direct sun or under bright lights. They make a dark car look hazy and take the depth out of the shine.

What causes them

  • Automatic car washes with brushes or dirty cloth strips.
  • Washing with a dirty sponge, a gritty towel, or without rinsing first.
  • Drying with rough towels, or wiping a dusty car with a dry cloth.
  • Poor polishing or buffing by someone who is not careful.

All of these drag tiny particles across the clear coat, leaving thousands of microscopic scratches that scatter light.

Why dark cars show them more

Dark paint reflects like a mirror, so any scratch stands out. Light-colored cars have swirls too. They just hide them better.

How they are removed

Swirl marks sit in the clear coat. A technician uses a machine polisher with a pad and polish to level the surface by removing a very thin amount of clear coat, so the scratches disappear and the paint is smooth again. This is called paint correction. The work usually starts with a thorough wash and decontamination, and a paint thickness gauge shows how much clear coat is available to work with.

What it cannot fix

  • Scratches deep enough to catch a fingernail may be reduced, but not fully removed.
  • Paint that is already thin, or has failed clear coat, may not be safe to polish heavily.

How to keep them from coming back

  • Use the two-bucket method and a clean microfiber mitt.
  • Rinse thoroughly before you touch the paint.
  • Avoid brush car washes.
  • Protect the corrected paint with a ceramic coating or paint protection film, which makes it easier to wash safely.
